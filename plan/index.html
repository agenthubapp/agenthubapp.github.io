FAQ

A few honest answers.

Is Free really free?

Yes. The free lane needs no key and no account — it's the default the app ships with.

What does Plan gate?

Ceilings, never tools. Every feature on this site works on Free; Plan raises the numbers above — review fan-out, running more than one workflow at once, failover and fast context. Sessions and history are already unlimited on Free.

Does it phone home?

No. A Plan key is checked offline, on your Mac, against a signature — there is no server call and no account tied to it.
